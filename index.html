Common Questions

Transparent answers before you reach out

If your question is not listed, please include it in your enquiry. Responses depend on availability and office hours.

How do I request a consultation? +

Use the enquiry form, call, or WhatsApp. Share a brief, non-confidential description of the matter type. The office will review and explain the next step, subject to availability and conflict checks.

What should I prepare before contacting the office? +

A short summary of facts, relevant dates, and general nature of documents available. Please do not attach confidential originals to the initial enquiry.

How are consultation fees communicated? +

Fees and scope are discussed during initial communication or at the consultation stage, before any commitment. No representation is assumed until agreed.

Can I request a remote consultation? +

Remote consultations may be available on request, depending on matter type and office schedule. Please indicate your preference in the form.

Which locations does the advocate serve? +

Based in New Delhi, operating from Chamber No. D-501A. Jurisdiction and forum depend on the nature of your matter and applicable law.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm representation? +

No. Submitting a form, calling, or messaging does not create an advocate-client relationship. Representation requires explicit acceptance after review.
